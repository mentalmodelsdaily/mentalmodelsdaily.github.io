This notice explains how Mental Models Daily ("we") handles personal data when you visit mentalmodelsdaily.com, join our email list or buy from us. We are the controller of that data. Contact: {{contact_email}}.

## What we collect and why

**When you browse.** We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits. It sets no cookies and does not identify you; we see totals such as page views and the countries and devices visitors use. Our host, Cloudflare, keeps short technical logs (such as IP addresses) to run and protect the site. Our legal basis is our legitimate interest in a secure, working website.

**When you join the email list** (the free printable, a "Notify me" request or the daily model), we keep your email address, the list you joined and when you confirmed. We only add you after you confirm by clicking the link in our first email. Our legal basis is your consent, which you can withdraw at any time with the unsubscribe link in every email. The list is run by MailerLite, which stores it in the European Union.

**When you buy the Core Deck**, the order is handled by Shopify, which collects your name, address, email and payment details to take payment and deliver it, and passes the delivery details to our printer. We never see your card details. Our legal basis is the contract with you. See Shopify's privacy policy for how it handles your data.

**When you buy a book on Amazon**, you are Amazon's customer and Amazon's privacy notice applies. We receive no personal data about you. Some of our Amazon links are affiliate links; see the affiliate disclosure.

**Podcast and video players.** Spotify and YouTube players load only if you press play and accept. Until then nothing is sent to them. Once loaded, their own privacy notices and cookies apply.

## How long we keep it

Email addresses: until you unsubscribe, then removed within 30 days (we keep a suppression record so we do not email you again). Order records: as long as the law requires for tax and accounting, usually six years. Technical logs: as long as Cloudflare keeps them, usually a few days.

## Where it goes

Our service providers (Cloudflare, MailerLite, Shopify and our printer) process data for us under contracts that protect it. Some of them may process it outside the UK; where they do, they use safeguards recognised by UK law, such as the UK International Data Transfer Addendum. We never sell your data.

## Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, to delete it, to restrict or object to our use of it, or to move it to another service, and you can withdraw consent at any time. Write to {{contact_email}}. If you are unhappy with how we have handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).
