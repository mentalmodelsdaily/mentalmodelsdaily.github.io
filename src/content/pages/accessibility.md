We want everyone to be able to use mentalmodelsdaily.com. We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA.

## What we do

- Every page works with a keyboard alone, and shows clearly where you are.
- Text and its background have enough contrast, in every colour scheme on the site.
- Every drawing has a text description, and the films have controls to pause them.
- Pages still work if you zoom to 400% or make the text larger.
- Animations stop if your device asks for reduced motion.
- Every page works without JavaScript.

## Known limits

The product films have no sound and no narration; everything they show is also described in text on the same page. Pages from the books, shown as pictures, are samples: the full text of each model's teaser is on its own page.

## Tell us

If something is hard to use, or you need any of our content in another format, write to {{contact_email}}. We aim to reply within five working days.
