## The Core Deck

Each deck is printed for you and posted from our printer. Delivery times and costs for your country are shown at checkout before you pay.

{{deck_shipping}}

## Changed your mind?

If you live in the UK or the EU you can cancel your order within 14 days of receiving it, without giving a reason. Tell us at {{contact_email}} with your order number, then post the deck back, unused and in its box, within 14 days of telling us. We refund the price and the standard delivery charge within 14 days of getting it back; return postage is yours to pay.

## Something wrong?

If your deck arrives damaged, misprinted or incomplete, email {{contact_email}} within 30 days with your order number and a photo. We will send a replacement or refund you in full, including postage. You do not need to send the faulty deck back.

## The Workbook and the Almanac

The books are printed and delivered by Amazon. Delivery, returns and refunds follow Amazon's policies for your store.
