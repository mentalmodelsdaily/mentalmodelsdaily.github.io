**We set no cookies.** Our analytics (Cloudflare Web Analytics) count visits without cookies, so there is no banner to dismiss.

## The one choice we ask you to make

Podcast and video players come from Spotify and YouTube, and they may set their own cookies. So they do not load until you press play. We then ask whether you accept: if you do, the player loads; if you do not, we give you a link to listen or watch on their site instead.

We remember your answer in your browser's local storage (an entry called `mmd-consent`), so we do not ask on every page. It holds only your choice, never anything about you, and it never leaves your device.

## Changing your mind

Use **Cookie settings** at the foot of any page to change your choice. You can also clear the site's data in your browser's settings.

## Buying

The Buy buttons take you to Shopify (the Core Deck) or Amazon (the books). Those sites use cookies of their own, explained in their policies.
